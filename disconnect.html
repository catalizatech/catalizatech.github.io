Disconnect

An authorized user of the connected QuickBooks Online company can revoke this integration's access at any time from within QuickBooks, by removing the app from the company's connected apps. Once access is revoked, the integration can no longer read or write any data in that company.

You can also ask us to disconnect and to delete the stored access credentials by writing to jahernandezdepaz@gmail.com. We will delete them promptly after the request.
