Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers a private integration operated by Catalizatech ("we") for a small number of related client organizations (each one, "the Client"). It connects only to the QuickBooks Online company of each Client that has authorized it. Each Client's data and credentials are kept separate from those of every other Client, and are never shared between Clients.

2. What we access

With the Client's authorization, the integration reads accounting data from the Client's QuickBooks Online company (such as the chart of accounts, transactions and reports) and records accounting transactions in it. We do not collect data from any other source or from any other users.

3. How we use it

We use this data only to prepare the Client's accounting reports and to record the Client's accounting transactions. We do not sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Access credentials (tokens) are kept in a protected file on a private computer under our control. They are never published or sent to third parties other than Intuit, as required to operate the connection. Accounting files used for processing are kept on that same private computer.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep credentials and working files only while the engagement with the Client lasts. When the Client revokes access, or asks us in writing to delete data, we delete the stored credentials and any working copies of the Client's data. Requests can be sent to the contact below.

6. Your rights

The Client may revoke access at any time from within QuickBooks (see the disconnect page) and may ask us what we hold and to delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
