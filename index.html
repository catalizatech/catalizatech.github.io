Catalizatech – Private QuickBooks Integration

This site belongs to a private integration operated by Catalizatech for a small number of related client organizations. The integration connects to each organization's own QuickBooks Online company, only with that organization's authorization, to read accounting reports and to record accounting transactions on its behalf.

It is not a public product, it is not offered to other users, and it is not listed on the QuickBooks App Store.
